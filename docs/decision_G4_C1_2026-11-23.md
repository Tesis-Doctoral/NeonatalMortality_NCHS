# Decision log entry — criterion C1 decided by the AUROC drop only (2026-11-23)

Spanish version: [`decision_G4_C1_2026-11-23_es.md`](decision_G4_C1_2026-11-23_es.md).

**Stage / gate:** G4, criterion C1. **Type of reason:** methodological (dependence of the AUPRC on prevalence).
**Validated by:** Emmanuel Gutiérrez Jiménez (to be confirmed by the thesis supervisor). **Recorded after** notebook 05 of this model was run: the change was prompted by its result.

## Context

Before this decision, C1 required two things at the landmark population: an AUROC drop of 0.05 or less **and** at least two thirds of the AUPRC gain over prevalence kept. In the neonatal model (M4), 55% of the deaths (34,466 of 62,713) occur on days 0 and 1. At the 48-hour landmark (decision M4-D1) the AUROC falls from 0.964 to 0.932 (drop 0.032; 95% CI 0.031–0.033), within the limit, but only 26% (25–27%) of the AUPRC gain is kept. The AUPRC, and therefore its gain over prevalence, depends on the prevalence, which falls by more than half when the deaths of the first two days are removed. The second condition thus penalizes an outcome with many early events even when the ranking of the remaining population stays good, which is what the AUROC drop measures.

## Rule (from 2026-11-23)

1. **C1 is decided by the AUROC drop at the landmark population: 0.05 or less.** The share of the AUPRC gain kept is still computed and reported with its 95% interval, but it does not decide.
2. Everything else in gate G4 and the thresholds of the other criteria do not change.

## Result for M4

Notebook 05 (`data/outputs_05_main_evaluation.txt`, gate G4): C1 met (drop 0.032), C2 met, C3 not applicable, C4 met. AUROC at the landmark 0.932 (≥ 0.75). **Classification: predictor.** With the previous rule C1 would not have been met (26% of the AUPRC gain kept) and the model would have been an early identifier; this entry is the only place where that is recorded.
