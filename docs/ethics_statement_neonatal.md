# Ethics statement — neonatal model NCHS (M4)

Spanish version: [`ethics_statement_neonatal_es.md`](ethics_statement_neonatal_es.md). Covers TRIPOD+AI item 17 (ethics approval and consent). Version of 2026-11-26.

## Data

- **Source:** public-use *Period/Cohort Linked Birth/Infant Death* files of the National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS, CDC), birth cohorts 2016 to 2023, downloaded from https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data_access/vitalstatsonline.htm.
- **No identifiers:** the public-use files contain no names, identification numbers, addresses, geography below the country, hospital or day of birth. Each record is an anonymous birth.
- **NCHS conditions of use:** statistical analysis only; no attempt to identify any person or establishment; no linkage with data that could identify anyone. The study complies: the data are not redistributed (the repository publishes only the code and aggregate outputs) and no report shows a cell with fewer than 10 cases.

## Classification of the study

- **United States:** the analysis of public, de-identified data is not human subjects research under the definition of the Common Rule (45 CFR 46.102), because there is no interaction with persons and no identifiable information.
- **Colombia:** under Resolution 8430 of 1993 of the Ministry of Health (article 11) it is **research without risk**: it uses retrospective documentary research techniques and makes no intervention or modification in persons.
- **Informed consent:** not applicable. The data are secondary, anonymous and public.
- **Patient and public involvement (item 19):** none.
